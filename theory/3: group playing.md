# Group Playing 101: listening
Learning to play in a group has three levels:
1. Self
2. Section
3. Ensemble

With practice, you can listen to yourself, your section and the ensemble all at once. It's easiest to learn this one step at a time!

## Self
* _intonation_: can you play in tune with a reference tone?
* _dynamics_: are you playing at a comfortable volume? Can you get louder and softer and stay in tune?
* _tone_: very subjective, but do you sound good? can you vary your tone?

## Section or partner
* listen to yourself and your neighbor at the same time, practice by switching your attention back and forth
* listen for intonation
* match dynamics, and be able get louder and quieter together

## Ensemble
Recognize and understand melody and supporting parts.
* Melody parts should stand out, but players need to listen to supporting parts to stay in tune and in time
* Supporting parts play behind the melody dynamically
